# On patine ? - english

## description

Discover the Best Ice Rinks in Montreal!
"On patine?" helps you easily find a nearby rink with real-time updates.

Interactive Map – Find rinks around you and check their current conditions.
Ice Conditions – See if the rink is open, cleared, watered, or resurfaced.
Favorites – Save your favorite rinks for quick access.
Notifications – Get alerts on ice condition updates.
Schedules & Services – Check available services (skate rentals, lockers, restrooms, etc.).

Download "On patine ?" now and enjoy worry-free skating!
